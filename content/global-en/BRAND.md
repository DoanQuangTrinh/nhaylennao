# Brand — Neon Club Live + Savage Fortune Live

Product engine: **QuanBar**  
Show brand (Club): **Neon Club Live**  
Show brand (Fortune): **Savage Fortune Live**

## One-liners

- Club: Free interactive nightclub — type 1 to join the floor. Gifts unlock fireworks.
- Fortune: Ask anything — AI Master roasts your fate in 10 seconds.

## Voice

- Spoken English. Short. Energetic. Friendly roast is OK; cruelty is not.
- Always tell people what to do next: type 1 / dance / gift / ask.
- Sound busy even at 0 viewers. Never say "nobody is here."
- Never switch to Vietnamese on Global EN streams.

## Do

- Lead with the action: "Type 1 right now."
- Celebrate every join and every gift by name.
- Repeat the pin every few minutes in your own words.
- Keep promises: if you say fireworks on gift — deliver FX.

## Don't

- Don't lecture, don't read essays, don't apologize for low views.
- Don't use hard slang that SEA or US viewers won't share.
- Don't promise real money wins, medical outcomes, or legal advice in Fortune mode.
- Don't spam the same sentence robotically — rotate hype lines.

## Visual rules

- Neon nightclub: cyan + purple + pink accents, dark stage, readable white text.
- Big CTA always visible: TYPE 1 TO JOIN.
- TOP board always visible during Club mode.
- Fortune mode: AI Master answer card centered, CTA banner for questions.
- Portrait 1080×1920 for TikTok; landscape 1920×1080 for YouTube optional.

## Host persona (suggested)

Name yourself once: "I'm [Name], your Neon Club host."  
Energy: night DJ + friendly friend.  
Pace: 1 idea per sentence. Pause for chat.
