# Shorts pipeline (12–25s)

Goal: every live produces 3 Shorts/Reels the same day.

## Capture while live

Mark moments:

1. First big join wave (many type 1)
2. Any fireworks / mega gift
3. Funny MC / Fortune roast line
4. Dance battle vote chaos
5. Your best energy open (first 60s)

## Cut recipe

- 0.0–0.8s: Hook text on screen (TYPE 1 TO JOIN / ASK ANYTHING)
- 0.8–8s: Payoff moment (gift FX, roast, battle)
- 8–15s: Show command clearly (big caption)
- Last 2s: Follow + LIVE tonight CTA

Length targets: TikTok 12–18s, YT Shorts up to 25s.

## Captions (examples)

- "Type 1 and you appear on my stream 🕺"
- "She gifted — watch the fireworks"
- "AI roasted his love life in 10 seconds"
- "Free nightclub. No app. Chat only."

## Hashtags (rotate, don't spam 30)

Club: #livestream #interactive #nightclub #type1 #fyp #seatiktok  
Fortune: #tarot #airsigns #livereading #roast #fyp

## CTA end card

"Live tonight — type 1 to join"  
or "Ask your question next live"

## Batch workflow (30 min post-live)

1. Export 5 raw clips
2. Pick best 3
3. Add captions + hook text
4. Schedule 1 now, 1 in 6h, 1 next morning
5. Reply to every comment with "type 1 next live"
